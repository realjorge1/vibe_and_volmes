# Bundled beats

The beat catalog is a typed manifest in [`data/beats/beatCatalog.ts`](../../data/beats/beatCatalog.ts);
the files are registered in [`data/beats/beatAssets.ts`](../../data/beats/beatAssets.ts).

## How they are made (beat engine v3)

The 42 `.m4a` loops (AAC, 192 kbps, stereo) are rendered by
[`scripts/beats/make_beats.py`](../../scripts/beats/make_beats.py):

- **8 bars per loop.** Bars 1–4 state the idea, bars 5–8 answer it with a
  variation, and bar 8 ends on a fill (snare/clap roll, toms, reverse cymbal)
  that throws back to bar 1. Loops run 11–27 s depending on tempo.
- **Sampled instruments** (pianos, Rhodes and FM electric pianos, clean and
  nylon guitars, strings, choir, kalimba, marimba, flute, brass, pads) come
  from the GeneralUser GS SoundFont, rendered dry with FluidSynth.
- **Synthesized** 808s (with glides), kicks, snares, claps, metallic hats with
  trap rolls, shakers, congas, log drums, supersaw pads and stabs, FM bells,
  leads and formant vocal chops.
- **Genre-authentic writing**: trap and drill half-time grooves with sliding
  808s, afrobeats swing with shakers, congas, highlife guitar and log drums,
  R&B extended-chord Rhodes with trap-soul or neo-soul drums, dance-pop and
  pop grooves, indie on a live (sampled) drum kit, hyperpop supersaws and
  crushed bells. Each beat seeds its own palette, progression, groove, hat
  rolls and 808 slides, so siblings in a genre sound different.
- **Mixed and mastered**: per-part EQ, compression and saturation, kick
  sidechain on pads and keys, plate/hall/room convolution reverbs, a
  tempo-synced ping-pong delay, glue compression, a true-peak limiter (-1.2 dBTP),
  loudness matched per genre (-10.5 to -13 LUFS).
- **Seamless looping**: every reverb, delay and note tail that runs past the
  loop end is folded back onto the start, so the file loops without a click.

```sh
py scripts/beats/fetch_tools.py     # once: FluidSynth, the SoundFont, ffmpeg -> scripts/beats/.tools/
py scripts/beats/make_beats.py      # all beats (rewrites data/beats/beatAssets.ts)
py scripts/beats/make_beats.py trap-paranoia --review --wav   # one beat + spectrogram + WAV in scripts/beats/.review/
py scripts/beats/analyze.py trap-paranoia                     # per-part levels in the final mix
```

The output is deterministic: a beat id always renders the same audio.

## Replacing a beat with your own file

Put a royalty-free, CC0 or self-produced loop under the matching genre folder
(`.m4a` or `.mp3`, stereo, loops cleanly) and point its line in
`data/beats/beatAssets.ts` at it. A beat with no registered asset still works
in the simulation; it just plays silently.

Folders: `trap/ drill/ afrobeats/ rnb/ pop/ indie/ hyperpop/`
